\section*{\normalsize \centering \textbf{ABSTRACT}}

\noindent 

This study addresses the restructuring of the ETL (Extract, Transform and Load) processes of the State Revenue 
Department of Paraíba (SEFAZ-PB), motivated by the limitations of the legacy architecture based on SQL Server 
Integration Services (SSIS) in meeting the demands of Big Data environments. In this context, the study aimed 
to propose a restructuring of SEFAZ-PB's ETL strategy, using the BDMalhas project as a case study. To this end, 
a new ETL system was developed based on an architecture using technologies from the Big Data ecosystem, such as 
Apache Airflow, Apache Spark, and ClickHouse. Its evaluation considered maintainability, performance, and disk 
space utilization metrics, as well as the limitations identified throughout the research. The research was 
conducted as a case study with an applied nature and a quantitative approach, involving the implementation of 
the proposed architecture and its evaluation through comparative experiments. The results showed a reduction in 
the structural complexity of the pipelines and in disk space utilization, as well as indicating potential 
improvements in load execution time, considering the limitations and differences between the experimental 
environments. It is concluded that the proposed restructuring represents a viable alternative for the evolution 
of SEFAZ-PB's ETL processes, providing maintainability gains and reduced disk space utilization, in addition 
to potential performance improvements and scalability, although the results are limited to the context of 
the BDMalhas project and the environment used in the research.

\vspace*{0.5cm}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} ETL; Big Data; data engineering; ETL restructuring; data architecture.
